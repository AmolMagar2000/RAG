# SAMPLE Sponsor Guidance (synthetic test data - NOT official UKVI guidance)

## 1 Right to Work Checks

1.1 Initial checks
A sponsor must complete a right to work check before employment starts and keep a copy of the evidence.

1.2 Follow-up checks
If an employee has time-limited permission to work, the sponsor must record the expiry date and complete a follow-up right to work check before that date.

## 2 Record Keeping

2.1 Documents to retain
A sponsor must keep right to work evidence and sponsored worker records for the duration of employment plus 2 years.

2.2 Accessibility of records
Records must be available for inspection and be stored securely.

## 3 Reporting Duties

3.1 Reportable events
A sponsor must report certain events through the sponsorship management system, including changes to a sponsored worker's circumstances.

3.4 Change of work location
A sponsor must report a change in the work location of a sponsored worker within 10 working days of the change taking effect.

3.5 Absence without permission
A sponsor must report a sponsored worker who has not attended for 10 consecutive working days without permission.
